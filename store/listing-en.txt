НАЗВАНИЕ / APP NAME:
Jamshed Cargo

КРАТКОЕ ОПИСАНИЕ / SHORT DESCRIPTION (71/80):
Track your cargo, estimate delivery costs and find warehouse addresses.

ПОЛНОЕ ОПИСАНИЕ / FULL DESCRIPTION:
Jamshed Cargo helps cargo customers follow parcels from China to Tajikistan. Add a tracking number and see delivery stages in one place.

Use the app to:
• check warehouse-confirmed status updates and shipment history;
• view estimated arrival dates when the cargo operator has configured them;
• estimate delivery costs by weight and, for truck, by volume — with each warehouse's own price;
• copy the China warehouse address with your personal customer code;
• find the pickup address and opening hours;
• show a parcel's QR code when collecting a ready shipment;
• read cargo news and offers and ask the helper cargo questions.

Warehouse staff update the shipment status. Dates and prices are estimates; the final charge is determined after weighing. Notifications and helper services depend on the cargo operator's configuration and your device permissions.

The app interface is available in Tajik and Russian. Sign in with your phone number and password to access your own parcels.
